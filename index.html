Highlights

This 2002 GMC Yukon XL 2500 SLE is a heavy-duty, three-quarter-ton full-size SUV powered by the big-block 8.1L (496ci) Vortec V8 backed by a four-speed automatic with tow/haul mode and the Autotrac push-button four-wheel-drive system. Finished in Storm Gray Metallic over a Graphite/Pewter cloth interior, it was optioned from the factory with the Autoride load-leveling suspension package, a power sunroof, off-road skid plates, side-step running boards, and the heavy-duty trailering package.

It has spent its life in New Mexico and is a dry, rust-free truck. It shows 195,913 miles and is accompanied by a clean Carfax report documenting two owners, no accidents or damage, and 21 service records — including a recent oil-and-filter change. The original Monroney window sticker (original retail of $45,499) and a later dealer window sticker are included and can be viewed in full below.

The Details

Exterior

The body is finished in Storm Gray Metallic (paint code 14U) and rides on a long-wheelbase XL (Suburban-length) body. Equipment includes a chrome grille surround, chrome front and rear bumpers, halogen fog lamps, automatic headlamp control, body-color side moldings with bright inserts, a black roof-mounted adjustable luggage carrier, side-step running boards, deep-tinted glass, and a rear liftgate with a flip-up lift-glass window. As a lifelong New Mexico vehicle, it has been spared road salt and humidity and remains dry and rust-free — see the undercarriage photos below. Photos are provided throughout this listing to show the truck from all angles.

Wheels, Suspension & Brakes

The Yukon XL rolls on 16-inch polished forged-aluminum wheels wearing LT245/75R16E tires, with a full-size spare mounted under the frame on a lockable winch-type carrier. This 2500-series truck uses an independent torsion-bar front suspension and a two-stage multi-leaf rear, and it carries the factory Autoride package, which adds electronically controlled, self-leveling rear shocks. Stopping is handled by power four-wheel disc brakes with four-wheel ABS.

Interior

The cabin is trimmed in custom Graphite/Pewter cloth and currently seats up to five across two rows, with reclining high-back front bucket seats and a 60/40 split-folding center bench. Note that the factory removable three-passenger third-row bench is not included with the truck. Both front seats are six-way power-adjustable. Appointments include automatic climate control with front and rear controls plus an auxiliary rear heater, an eight-speaker sound system with subwoofer, an AM/FM stereo with CD and cassette, rear-seat audio controls, remote keyless entry, cruise control, power windows and locks, and an electrochromic rearview mirror with a compass and outside-temperature display.

Instruments & Electronics

A leather-wrapped tilt steering wheel frames analog instrumentation including a speedometer, tachometer, trip odometer, fuel level, voltmeter, oil pressure, engine-hour meter, and engine temperature, along with a Driver Information Center monitoring 18 systems. The digital odometer reads 195,913 miles. Note that the fuel gauge is no longer functioning (see seller's note below).

Engine & Drivetrain

Power comes from the 8.1L (496ci) Vortec V8, which was factory-rated at 340 horsepower and 455 lb-ft of torque — GM's biggest gas engine of the era. It pairs with a heavy-duty four-speed automatic transmission with overdrive and a tow/haul mode, an auxiliary transmission oil cooler, and the Autotrac electronic transfer case with dash-mounted push-button two- and four-wheel-drive selection. A 3.73 rear axle with a locking differential, an 8,600-lb GVWR, and the heavy-duty trailering package (weight-distributing hitch platform and seven-pin wiring) make this a capable tow vehicle, with a towing capacity of roughly 10,100 lbs — upgradable to about 12,000 lbs by swapping to a 4.10 axle ratio. A Prodigy electronic trailer-brake controller is fitted under the dash.
